# Changelog

## 1.2.0

- New look: chibi pixel-art night dragons with big green eyes, ear flaps and bat wings.
- The view now lives in the **Explorer** on a Stardew-style pixel meadow.
- Keep a whole herd (up to 8): hatch, release, rename and recolor dragons from the view title bar.
- Pick any color — 6 presets plus a custom hex color.
- Dragons wander, hop, sleep, and race for food when you double-click the grass.
- Cleaner line icons in the view.

## 1.1.0

- Pixel-art dragon in its own Activity Bar view, with feed / play / fire, XP and levels.
